it is `private: true`, not published, and not part of the deployed
observability product (`apps/web`, `apps/api`, `apps/proxy`, `apps/worker`).

**Triage verdict — DROP.** Per the SKILL.md step-4 drop rule:

> Drop the finding if it's in `test/`, `mock/`, `fixture/`, `example/`, `demo/`,
> `bench/`, `docs/`.

`apps/sample/` is the sample/demo of the same family. Bumping `next` here is
cosmetic — it doesn't reduce exposure on any deployed Superlog surface, only
on a private telemetry-fixture app. Filing a public PR for a demo-only dep
bump is the kind of low-signal noise the disclosure-channel routing is meant
to avoid. No PR opened.
