## Fix ownership

Operator-gated. Two candidate paths (from ISS-029 postmortem):

1. Top up usepod.ai payment or add the `X-PAYMENT`/`PAYMENT-SIGNATURE` header the
   endpoint now requires (temporary — will re-fire when quota drains again).
2. Rotate fleet to backup LLM proxy — flip `gateway.provider` in aeon.yml from
   `direct` to `bankr` or `virtuals`, or investigate why `direct` is silently
   routing through usepod despite the config.

Aeon cannot self-repair — failure surface is the LLM proxy Aeon runs on.
